Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Çengelish has no accounts. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number, we show no ads and we don't track you.

Data on your device

Your nickname, avatar, puzzle progress, handwriting, word notebook, streak and badges are stored only on your device. We never see them.

iCloud backup (optional)

If you turn on iCloud backup, this data is stored in your private iCloud area through Apple's CloudKit. Only you can access it; we can't.

Puzzle content

New puzzles and word packs are downloaded from Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). No personal data is sent. Firebase App Check uses Apple's App Attest to confirm the app is genuine; this does not identify you. Like any internet request, Firebase servers may briefly process your IP address.

Purchases

Subscriptions and pack purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see payment details.

Notifications

Daily reminders and streak notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

Deleting your data

Settings → Data & backup → Delete all data removes everything on the device. Your iCloud backup can be deleted in iOS Settings → iCloud → Manage Storage.
