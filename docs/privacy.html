Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Çengelish has no accounts. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number, we show no ads and we don't track you.

Data on your device

Your nickname, profile photo, puzzle progress, handwriting, word notebook, streak and badges are stored only on your device. We never see them.

iCloud backup (optional)

If you turn on iCloud backup, this data is stored in your private iCloud area through Apple's CloudKit. Only you can access it; we can't.

Puzzle content

All puzzles, word packs and daily puzzles are built into the app. The app never goes online for content, so you can play offline.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, an anonymous crash report is sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can find and fix the bug. It contains technical details from the moment of the crash (stack trace, app version, device model, iOS version and state such as free memory) and a random identifier Crashlytics creates for this installation. Your nickname, profile photo, puzzle content or any other personal data are never sent; the report does not identify you and is not used for ads or tracking. Like any internet request, Firebase servers may briefly process your IP address.

Purchases

Çengelish Plus and pack purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see payment details.

Notifications

Daily reminders and streak notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

Deleting your data

Settings → Data & backup → Delete all data removes everything on the device. Your iCloud backup can be deleted in iOS Settings → iCloud → Manage Storage.
